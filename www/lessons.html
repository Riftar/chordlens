Build the chord. Hear the key.

Two lessons, self-paced: what a triad actually is, and the seven chords every major key hands you. Everything plays through the same synth that renders the test fixtures - one engine, zero downloads.

Lesson 1 - What a chord is

A triad is three notes: root, third, fifth. The third decides the mood - major bright, minor dark, diminished unstable. Build one, then flip the quality and play again.

Lesson 2 - Every chord in a key

A major key gives you exactly seven chords, one per scale degree. The pattern is identical in every key - only the names change. Tap any card to hear it; then hear the strongest gravity in music: V pulling home to I.

Tonic I iii vi - home, rest Subdominant ii IV - moves away Dominant V vii° - tension, resolves to I
